## Phase 0 — Pronunciation (2 weeks, ~10 h)

**No characters, no grammar. Sound only.**

**Week 1:** tones in isolation → `z/c/s` vs `zh/ch/sh` vs `j/q/x` → `-n` vs `-ng`, `ü`
**Week 2:** all 20 tone-pair combinations with real words (中国 Zhōngguó 1+2, 老师 lǎoshī 3+1, 电话 diànhuà 4+4, 谢谢 xièxie 4+neutral), then sandhi: 3+3→2+3 (你好 = *ní hǎo*), 不 bù→bú before 4th tone.

**Daily 35 min:** 10 min listening discrimination → 15 min producing + recording yourself → 10 min review.

**Your traps as a Vietnamese speaker:**
- Tones do NOT map to Vietnamese tones. T4 is a sharp forceful drop, not huyền. T3 in speech is just **low**.
- `zh/ch/sh/r` are retroflex — you'll substitute Vietnamese `tr/ch/s/r`.
- `-n` vs `-ng` — you'll import Vietnamese rules. Drill `an/ang`, `en/eng`, `in/ing`.
- `ju/qu/xu/yu` are secretly `ü`.
- Spelling lies: `-ui` = `-uei`, `-iu` = `-iou`, `-un` = `-uen`.

**End of week 2: book one 30-min italki session (~$6) for a pronunciation check only.** A native ear catches in 10 minutes what you can't hear in 6 months.

**Verify before moving on:**
- [ ] Hear a random syllable, name its tone, 9/10
- [ ] Produce `zi / zhi / ji` distinctly on demand
- [ ] Tutor confirms no fossilizing errors

## Phase 1 — The lesson cycle

6 sessions per lesson. **Every session starts with 10 min flashcards.**

### S1 — Blind listening + vocab (45 min)
1. Dialogue audio 3×, **book closed**. Note only: how many speakers, where, what mood.
2. Warm-up section (Khởi động).
3. Vocab list + audio. Write the **Hán-Việt** next to each word (银行 → ngân hàng, 学习 → học tập). This is your unfair advantage — build it from lesson 1.
4. Make 8–12 cards. **Front = audio + full sentence.** Never word→meaning alone.
5. Replay audio, book still closed.

### S2 — Grammar + decode + video (60 min)
1. Grammar section. **Write 3 sentences of your own** per point, about your real life. Mark unsure ones `?`.
2. Read the dialogue. **10 seconds per sentence, then check.** Guess compounds from known characters (火车站 = fire + vehicle + station → train station). Don't guess unknown characters or idioms — look those up.
3. Read aloud once, slowly.
4. **NOW watch the lesson walkthrough video** (15–20 min). Never before steps 1–3 — watching first kills the blind listening and the attempt-then-correct effect. One pass only.

### S3 — Drills (45 min)
1. Msutong exercises. **Listening first**, written after.
2. Whatever you got wrong → open HSK, find the same point, drill it. That's HSK's only job.

### S4 — Shadowing (40 min)
A. Silent read-along 2× → B. Aloud with audio 0.75×, text visible 3–5× → C. Same at 1.0× 5× → D. 1-second lag, text visible 5× → E. **No text** 3×

**Then record stage E.** Compare to native audio. Fix the **single worst thing** only. Re-record.

Don't live at 0.75×.

### S5 — Handwriting (45 min)
Stroke order rules (once): top→bottom, left→right · horizontal before crossing vertical (十) · outside before inside, **close the box last** (国) · center before sides (小) · left-falling before right-falling (人)

Per character: watch animation → write 3× saying it aloud → **cover, write from memory, check** → next day recall before writing.

Retrieval, not repetition. Type the sentence exercises; don't handwrite those.

### S6 — Output (50–70 min)
1. **Rewrite the dialogue with your own facts.** Same patterns, your life.
   **Recombine, don't expand — do not google new words.** Wrong collocations and measure words drilled in are worse than boring sentences. Use words from earlier lessons.
2. **Say it out loud, no notes, recorded.** Stall >3 sec = back to S4.
3. From month 2: bring it to your weekly tutor. Say *"force me to use this grammar point."*
4. Anything you wanted to say but couldn't → straight into flashcards.

**Gap days:** flashcards, 10 min. That's it.

## Verify — the exit test

**Do not advance because you finished the pages.** Advance when, cold, book closed:

- [ ] Understand the dialogue audio at 1.0× on first play → *fail = redo S4*
- [ ] 3 sentences with the new grammar, about your life, within 10 sec each → *fail = redo S6*
- [ ] Recognize ~90% of the new characters → *fail = carry forward, it catches up*
- [ ] Handwrite the lesson's characters from memory → *fail = redo S5*

**Every 5 lessons — one session, no new material:** re-shadow old dialogues cold, redo failed exercises, skim the HSK list and add only what you can't produce.

## Milestones

- [ ] **Month 3** — introduce yourself, your job, your daily schedule. 2 min unscripted.
- [ ] **Month 6** — order food, ask directions, handle a shop transaction. Survive a 10-min tutor call.
- [ ] **Month 10** — explain what you do at work to a stranger. ~60% of a slow podcast.
- [ ] **Month 15** — 30-min conversation on familiar topics, with repair strategies (听不懂，能再说一遍吗？ *I don't understand, could you say it again?*)

## The non-negotiable

**From month 2: one tutor session per week** (italki, ~$5–8/hr). 30 minutes of forced production beats 5 extra hours of reading. Skip this and you'll finish Sơ cấp 4 and still freeze in a real conversation.

---

## Don't

1. Read the text before hearing it — permanently caps your listening
2. Skip the recordings — you fossilize errors you can't perceive
3. Word-only flashcards — 800 words, zero speech
4. Watch the walkthrough before attempting the lesson
5. Advance on page count instead of the exit test
6. Bulk flashcards once a week
7. Google vocabulary for your practice sentences

---

## Radicals — the approach

Don't memorize 214 upfront. Learn ~50 actively over 3 months, the rest **as they appear** in words you're actually learning.

Start with these 12: 亻person · 口 mouth · 女 woman · 忄heart · 扌hand · 氵water · 木 tree · 日 sun · 讠speech · 辶 movement · 艹 grass · 宀 roof

**The real accelerator — sound components.** ~80% of characters have one part hinting at meaning, one at sound: 青 qīng → 请 qǐng, 清 qīng, 情 qíng. Kicks in around your 150th character. Watch for it.
